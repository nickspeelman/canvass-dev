Canvas

An experimental touch-drawing instrument

Canvas grew out of a simple paint program I made for a friend’s kid. While watching it in use, I noticed unexpected effects emerging from multitouch gestures and a handful of simple drawing rules.

I decided to lean into those accidents and turn the idea into a broader creative tool for teens and grown-ups. Canvas is the result: a drawing instrument built around surprising interactions between simple sets of rules.

If you have issues, questions or other feedback, you can contact me at nick@nickspeelman.com
